Privacy

What we keep, and how to delete it.

Draft, pending legal review (October 2026). CarbonLedger is a test-network pilot. Units are pilot units, not yet verified credits, and no payment is taken.

Without signing in

Using the app gives your browser an anonymous login. When you retire pilot units, the public ledger records a receipt with that login's id, the grams, the kind (shopping, travel or daily), the country you chose, a serial range and the time. What you bought or where you went is not stored: only a fingerprint (hash) of it is kept, so the receipt can be checked. Receipts are public and permanent, because a ledger cannot forget a retirement. Only fingerprints go on Solana, never names, places or baskets.

If you save your progress

You give us an email address. We use it only to send a one-time sign-in link (it works once, for 15 minutes) and to join your logins into one account. The email is sent through our email provider, Resend. If you choose a public name, it is shown on the weekly league; otherwise you appear as a random name such as "Player 7F3A". A business may add its name and staff count, which head its retirement statement.

Counting visits

We count how many times a day the website and the app are opened, a scan is started, a receipt is checked or the app is installed. We keep the day, the name of the event and a number. No cookie, no id, no address and no device details are kept, and no third-party tracker runs on these pages. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, nothing is counted.

How long, and deleting

Sign-in links last 15 minutes. Your email and public name stay until you delete them. In the app, open Me → Your account → Delete my data: your email, name and sign-in links are deleted at once and your logins are switched off on every device. Your receipts stay on the ledger with an id that no longer leads to you.

Where it is kept

On our servers at Railway, in a Postgres database. We do not sell or share personal data. Questions: message @Carbon_Ledger until a dedicated privacy address is published here.
