# Brief for counsel: questions before CarbonLedger (to be renamed CarbonCoin) takes real money

Written 2026-11-15 by the founders' team. Please answer each question, or tell us which specialist to ask.

## What the product does
A person or a business works out the CO2 of a basket, a journey or a period, and pays to retire carbon units against
it. Each retirement takes a fresh serial range and gets a receipt whose hash is recorded on the Solana blockchain. The
receipt token is non-transferable and has no price after the sale. Nobody can resell or trade it.

## The questions
1. **Green claims (rules/COMPLIANCE.md C5).** Once the units are real certified credits retired on the buyer's behalf,
   what may we say on screen and in adverts? We currently avoid "offset", "carbon neutral", "net zero" and "climate
   positive". Which of these, if any, may a buyer use after a retirement, and with what wording?
2. **The name "CarbonCoin" and the token (C6).** Under UK financial promotion rules, is a non-transferable,
   non-tradeable retirement receipt a cryptoasset? Does selling it under the brand "CarbonCoin" change the answer?
   Could any version be treated as an investment? Is the CarbonCoin trademark clear for this use?
3. **Taking payments (C10).** Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and bank through Stripe; USDC through Solana Pay. Terms of
   sale, refunds after a retirement (which cannot be undone), consumer cancellation rights, VAT, and anti-money-
   laundering duties for stablecoin payments.
4. **Supply.** If we buy certified credits (for example biochar credits) and retire them for customers, what must the
   supplier contract say about resale, retirement on behalf of others, and naming the beneficiary?
5. **Personal data (C8).** Users' baskets and trips; in the CharHarvest pilot, farmers' GPS-tagged photos. Lawful
   basis, retention, deletion, and sharing with a verifier. Only hashes go on chain.
6. **Data licences (C13).** Open Food Facts (ODbL), Our World in Data, Agribalyse and Poore and Nemecek: the credit
   lines and share-alike duties for a commercial app.
7. **Children (C9).** Do we need an age check before payments, and what defaults apply if under-18s use the game?

## What we have already done
Test-mode payments only (no real money), the pilot-units label on every screen and response, refusals kept on the
record, and personal data kept off chain. See docs/GO-LIVE.md for the full plan.
