Your AI agent will never fail its own test.

It writes the work.
It writes the report.
It marks the report.

That is not proof. That is a story.

Before you trust an agent with real work, ask it three things:

1) What did you do?
2) What were you refused?
3) Who can check?

If the answers live only in its own log, you have its word.

Its word is not a record.

A record is written as it happens, by something the agent cannot edit.

Ask the third question first. It tells you the most.
